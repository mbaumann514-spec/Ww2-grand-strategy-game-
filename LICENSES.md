# Licences

This game is intended to be sold. **Anything that cannot be shipped commercially does not go in.** Every third-party
thing the game contains or is built with is listed here with its licence and how it is used, in the same commit that
brings it in. The house rules, taken over from the sister game *Sacrum Imperium* and stricter than the law in places:

1. Data and assets that ship are public domain or permissively licensed. No GPL, no GADM, no OpenStreetMap, no
   geoBoundaries, nothing share-alike.
2. No image file in the project. Everything visible is drawn in code or written into it as text.
3. No AI-generated pictures (the author's exception for PixelLab is not used by this game so far: it has no sprites).
4. Fonts only under the SIL Open Font License.
5. No sign, slogan, song or speech of the National Socialist or Fascist regimes, and no emblem of any party or
   ideology at all (docs/pruefer/anwalt_vorpruefung.md).

### History -- facts, not text

The borders of 1 September 1939 (`tools/karte/regeln_1939.py`), the armies, industry, oil, fortified lines and dates
(`data/scenarios/1939/scenario.json`, `data/countries.json`) are facts, researched by the `historiker` reviewer and
written down in `docs/pruefer/historiker_vorpruefung_1939.md` with their sources (Harrison, *The Economics of World War
II*, 1998; Maddison; Bairoch 1982; Kennedy 1987; Zaloga; Müller-Hillebrand; Niehorster; and the pages listed there).
Figures and dates are not protected; no prose, table or order of battle of anybody else's is copied, and where a
figure is our own estimate the data says *(filled in)*. No atlas was traced and no other game's map, region division,
unit values, names of mechanics or texts were used.
