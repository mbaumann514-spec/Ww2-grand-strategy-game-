# Finis Pacis (working title)

A grand strategy game about the Second World War in Europe, North Africa and the Middle East, from 1 September 1939,
for Android. The sister of *Sacrum Imperium*. Private.

- `CLAUDE.md` — how to work here: the author's rules and the commands that prove a package is finished.
- `docs/ROADMAP.md` — what is built, what was measured, what comes next.
- `docs/pruefer/` — the historian's and the lawyer's pre-checks.
- `LICENSES.md` — every source and its licence.

To play on a desk: `gradle :lwjgl3:run` (Java 21). Tap a region, "March", tap where they shall go; the triangle
starts the clock.
